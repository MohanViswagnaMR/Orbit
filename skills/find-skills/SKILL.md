# Finding skills that could help

Orbit's collection holds more skills than you were given: other people's, new ones from GitHub, the owner's own.

Orbit already searches it for each turn and lists the best matches in your briefing ("Skills in Orbit's collection that may help with this"). Check those first. Search yourself when the work turns out to be different from what it said, when it has several parts, or before you hand work out.

1. **Search.** Call find_skills with a few words about the work ("fill in a pdf form", "landing page copy", "sql report"). Try a second wording if the first finds nothing useful.
2. **Use what fits.**
   - You have it: use it with the Skill tool (orbit:<name>).
   - You don't have it: read_skill gives you its instructions. Follow them for this task.
   - Handing the work out: give it to someone who has the skill, or name the skill in the brief so they can read_skill it.
3. **No good match:** just do the work. Never force a skill that doesn't fit.

Keep it quick: a search or two, not a research project. Skip it for quick answers, small edits and chit-chat.

If a skill you don't have helps you, say so in your reply so the owner can give it to you for next time.
