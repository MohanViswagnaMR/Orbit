# Checking a teammate's work

## Check

- Compare it with what "done" meant in your brief. Is everything there?
- Look at the real thing, not just the summary: open the files they made, run the tests or the script if you can, spot-check facts and numbers.
- Watch for: missing parts, guesses presented as facts, broken files, and work nobody asked for.

## Decide

- **Good enough:** accept it and move on. Don't polish what already does the job.
- **Small gaps you can fix in a minute:** fix them yourself.
- **Wrong or incomplete:** create a follow-up task with specific feedback: what's wrong, what to keep, what done looks like. Keep the same model if it was a misunderstanding; go one model up if the work was too hard for it (choose-model).
- **Wrong person:** give it to someone who fits better.
- **It failed** (Orbit says the subtask failed): read why. If the model wasn't up to it, move up (choose-model). If something is missing (access, files, a decision), fix the cause or ask.

## Limits

At most two retries for any piece of work. After that, stop and report the blocker to whoever asked, with what you tried. Never loop.
