Orbit isn't running

Orbit runs on this computer and normally starts by itself when you log in. Your team, chats and files are all safe; the app just isn't answering right now.

  1. Start it again with the command for your computer:
    launchctl kickstart -k gui/$(id -u)/local.orbit 2>/dev/null || launchctl kickstart -k gui/$(id -u)/local.crew
    Start-ScheduledTask -TaskName Orbit
    systemctl --user restart orbit
  2. Still not back? Start it by hand: open a terminal in the folder where Orbit is installed and run node server.js. Keep that window open.
  3. To see what went wrong, look at server.log in Orbit's data folder (~/.orbit unless you chose another).
